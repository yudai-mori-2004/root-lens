# Child Safety Standards

We do not permit children to be recorded as subjects. Recording staff must be at least 18 years old.

All recorded data is reviewed before it is provided to a recipient. Data containing children is deleted without being provided. If we identify content constituting child sexual abuse material, we report it to law enforcement in accordance with applicable law.

If you have a concern, contact contact@rootlens.io.
